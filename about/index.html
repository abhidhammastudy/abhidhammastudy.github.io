About

A reading companion to the Theravāda Abhidhamma, made as a study aid and not as an authority.

New to the subject? Where to begin gives a short first path and a map of the whole journey.

What it follows

The order and the numbers follow Bhikkhu Bodhi's A Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma. The site retells and cites the Manual; it does not reproduce its text or tables, which belong to the Buddhist Publication Society. References like "IV §6" are to its chapters and sections, so you can read the source alongside.

Suttas are cited by their SuttaCentral numbers. Short sutta passages are retold, or quoted from Bhikkhu Sujato's translations, which he has placed in the public domain.

What is commentarial

Much of the Abhidhamma's system, such as the seventeen-moment process or the count of thirty-one planes, comes from the commentaries rather than the canonical books. Each concept page says so in "In the texts". Readings of stories are marked as study aids.

Reflect and discuss

Each concept and reading ends with an opening question. Under it is a box for your own answer: write before you read the model answer. Your reflections are private. They are kept in your browser, and, if you sign in, in your account too, so they follow you between devices. My reflections collects them and lets you download them.

Below that is the page's discussion. Anyone can read it. To write, sign in with Google, GitHub or an email address. You can edit or delete your own comments at any time.

Privacy

The site keeps only what you give it: your name, the email you sign in with, your comments (public) and your reflections (private to you). It uses no analytics and no advertising. Deleting your account from your account page deletes your profile, comments and synced reflections. Reflections kept only in your browser never leave it.
