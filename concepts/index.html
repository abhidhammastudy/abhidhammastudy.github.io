Eighteen ideas

The Abhidhamma's many lists rest on a few ideas. Each page says what the idea is, how it connects to the others, where it comes from in the texts, and what to ask of it. The same ideas are drawn as a clickable map.

What there is

The realities the analysis ends in.

Two truths conventional, ultimate

Two ways of speaking: of persons and things, and of the ultimate dhammas they resolve into. The starting point of the Abhidhamma.
Citta consciousness

Consciousness: one moment of knowing an object. The Abhidhamma counts 89 kinds.
Cetasika mental factors

The mental factors that colour each citta.
Rūpa matter

Matter: 28 kinds, always arising in groups of eight or more.
Nibbāna unconditioned

The one unconditioned reality, known in the moments of awakening called path and fruition.

How it unfolds

Moments, processes, conditions and lives.

Life-continuum bhavaṅga

The quiet stream of mind that runs whenever nothing is actively being known, as in dreamless sleep.
Cognitive process 17 mind-moments

The fixed sequence in which cittas take an object.
Javana impulsion

The active phase of a cognitive process, where volition becomes kamma (except in an arahant).
24 conditions Paṭṭhāna

The ways in which one dhamma supports another.
Dependent origination 12 links, 3 lives

The twelve links, read across three lives.
Kamma volition

Volition, and the way it ripens.
Death and rebirth rebirth-linking

The last citta of one life and the first of the next, with nothing passing between.
31 planes worlds of mind

The worlds, matched to levels of mind.

The way out

Calm, insight, and the moment of the path.

The four noble truths ariya-sacca

The Buddha's four truths, which the Abhidhamma reads as a sorting of every mundane reality, with Nibbāna and the supramundane path set apart.
The noble eightfold path ariya aṭṭhaṅgika magga

Eight factors, right view to right concentration. In the strict sense, eight mental factors arising together in the moment of a supramundane path.
Calm samatha

Training the mind to rest on one object, from access concentration to the five jhānas, the meditative absorptions.
Insight vipassanā

The knowledges that see mind and matter rise and fall.
Path and fruition four paths

The four moments of awakening that cut defilements for good, each followed by its fruition.

A study companion to Bhikkhu Bodhi's A Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma, which it retells and cites but does not reproduce. Every page names its sources; the readings of stories are marked as study aids.
