Nutriment

āhāra

The four nutriments sustain what they feed: edible food the body; contact, volition and consciousness the states they arise with.

Direction

Manual

In depth

Nutriment condition, āhāra-paccayaāhāra · nutriment (four) edible food, contact, volition and consciousness, each sustaining its results, is help by sustaining. A nutriment produces what it feeds, keeps it going and supports its growth; the Manual's Guide makes its essential work reinforcement. There are four nutriments, one material and three mental.

Material nutriment is the nutritive essence, ojāojā · nutritive essence the essence in food, and in every kalāpa, that sustains matter, in edible food. Once swallowed, it produces new nutriment-born matter and reinforces the groups of all four origins already in the body, keeping them fresh enough to go on producing their kind. The essence already present inside every group also supports the rest of its own group and the groups around it.

The mental nutriments are contact, volition and consciousness. Each sustains the citta and factors it arises with and the matter arising with them. The suttas present the same four as what keep beings in existence and carry them toward a further birth, and the commentaries say what each one feeds: contact the three kinds of feeling, volition renewed existence, consciousness mind-and-matter. Nutriment overlaps with presence: food sustains the body only while its essence is present, and the Manual lists nutriment as one of the five kinds of presence.

What conditions
the nutritive essence in food and in every group of matter; and the three mental nutriments, contact, volition and consciousness
What is conditioned
nutriment-born matter, and the other matter of the body; and the cittas, factors and matter arising with each mental nutriment

An example. After a long walk you eat a bowl of rice. The nutritive essence in it produces new matter and props up the tired body; meanwhile, in the cittas that relish the taste, contact sustains the pleasant feeling that arises with it.

Its kinds

  • material nutriment. Nutritive essence, from food or within the body's own groups, produces and sustains matter.
  • mental nutriment. Contact, volition and consciousness sustain the mind and matter arising with them.

A prop set against an old house keeps it from falling. Nutriment holds up what already exists, so that it can go on.

Simile, after CMA VIII §23 Guide

Not to be confused with

  • nutritive essence. Ojā is a kind of matter. Nutriment condition is the force with which it, and three mental factors, sustain what they feed.
  • faculty. A faculty controls its domain. A nutriment sustains and strengthens. Consciousness is both a faculty and a nutriment, in two different roles.

Which way it works

  • matter → matter. Edible food nourishes this body.
  • mind → mind. The three mental nutriments, contact, volition and consciousness, sustain the mind they arise with.
  • mind → matter. The mental nutriments also sustain the matter that arises with them.

The same factors, as a category

In chapter VII, the nutriments (āhāra) work as this condition. Material nutriment sustains the body; the three mental nutriments sustain the states they arise with and mind-born matter.
