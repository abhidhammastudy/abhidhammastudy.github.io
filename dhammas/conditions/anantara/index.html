Condition · 4 of 24

Proximity

anantara

Each citta, as it ceases, makes way for the next and determines what kind can follow, so the stream of mind has no gaps.

Direction
Mind → mind
Group
Mind → mind
Manual
VIII §§11–28

In depth

Proximity condition, anantara-paccayaanantara · proximity Each citta, as it ceases, makes way for the next and determines what kind can follow, so the stream of mind has no gaps., is the help a citta gives, as it ceases, to the citta that arises immediately after it. The ceasing citta, with its factors, makes the next arise with nothing in between. It also settles what kind of citta may come next: after five-door adverting there must be a sense consciousness, after seeing a receiving citta, and so on. This is why the mind's processes run in a fixed order.

It is strictly mind to mind. Every citta and its factors condition the next in this way, with one exception: the death citta of an Arahant, which is followed by nothing. Even across the edge of a life the relation holds: an ordinary being's death citta is the proximity condition for the rebirth citta of the next existence.

Four conditions share exactly the same conditioning and conditioned states. Contiguity is the same relation in other words. Absence and disappearance describe the same help as a ceasing that gives room. Proximity decisive support is the same again, seen as the strong dependence of the next citta on the one before. The Manual places all of these under decisive support when it folds the twenty-four into four.

What conditions
every citta and its factors that has just ceased, except the death citta of an Arahant
What is conditioned
the citta and factors arising immediately after

An example. Hearing your name called: the life-continuum ceases and five-door adverting arises at once, then ear-consciousness, receiving, investigating, determining and the javanas. Each one ceases and makes way for the next in its proper order.

Not to be confused with

  • contiguity. Identical in meaning and in states; the names differ only in emphasis.
  • repetition. Repetition is narrower: only one mundane javana for the next javana of the same kind, adding strength. Proximity applies to every pair of successive cittas.
