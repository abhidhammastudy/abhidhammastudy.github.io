In depth

Presence condition, atthi-paccayaatthi · presence Presence is help by being there at the same time, without which the other could not stand., is help by being there. A state helps others simply by existing at the same time as they do: it lets them arise, or keeps them going. They need not arise together; it is enough that they overlap in time, and that the one supports the other while they do.

The Manual gives five kinds, each gathering other conditions under it. Conascence presence covers all that arise together. Prenascence presence covers the bases and present objects that arose before the citta. Postnascence presence covers later cittas helping the earlier body. Nutriment presence is the essence in food sustaining the body. Faculty presence is the material life faculty sustaining its group.

Presence is the largest of the four conditions into which the Manual folds all twenty-four. Eleven conditions always fall within presence: root, conascence, mutuality, result, nutriment, faculty, jhāna, path, association, non-disappearance and postnascence. Support, prenascence and dissociation fall within it too, though some of their kinds also count as object or decisive support; predominance and kamma fall within it only in their conascent kinds (Guide to VIII §27, after Ledi Sayadaw). What lies outside presence is what helps from a distance in time: objects of the past and future, the citta just ceased, past kamma, and the timeless: concepts and Nibbāna (Ledi Sayadaw, Paṭṭhānuddesa-dīpanī, Paccayasabhāga; Vibhāvinī on Saṅgaha VIII §34 (CS), which is CMA §26). Presence and non-disappearance are identical in meaning.

What conditions
states that coexist with what they help: conascent states; bases and objects that arose earlier; later cittas; nutritive essence; the material life faculty
What is conditioned
the states they help while overlapping: conascent mind and matter; cittas resting on bases or taking present objects; the body that arose earlier; matter sustained by nutriment; kamma-born matter of the life faculty's group

An example. Holding a cup of tea: the eye-sensitivity and the colour of the cup, already arisen, and the citta with its factors, all present at once, sustain the seeing; the food eaten at lunch, still present in the body, sustains the hand.

Its kinds

  • conascence presence. States arising together help one another while present.
  • prenascence presence. Bases and objects already arisen help the cittas that arise while they still stand.
  • postnascence presence. Later cittas help the earlier body while both exist.
  • nutriment presence. Nutritive essence sustains the body while it is present.
  • faculty presence. The material life faculty keeps its group alive while present.

Not to be confused with

  • conascence. Conascence requires arising together. Presence requires only overlap, so a body arisen earlier can be helped by a later citta.
  • absence. Absence helps by being gone; presence helps by still being here.

Which way it works

  • mind → mind. A citta and its factors help each other by being present together.
  • mind → matter. Present cittas sustain matter, both what arises with them and the body arisen earlier.
  • matter → matter. Present great elements, food and the life faculty sustain matter.
  • matter → mind. Present bases and objects support the cittas that depend on them.
