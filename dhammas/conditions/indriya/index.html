Faculty

indriya

A faculty controls its own domain, as a governor rules a province: the sense faculties, the life faculties, and the mental faculties such as faith and wisdom.

Manual

In depth

Faculty condition, indriya-paccayaindriya · faculty a dhamma that governs its own domain; 22 faculties, and condition 16, is help by control. Each faculty governs one domain and does not interfere with the others. The Manual gives three kinds.

The five sensitivities are prenascence faculties: each controls its own sense consciousness and the seven universal factors with it. Sharp eyes give clear seeing; failing eyes, blurred seeing. The material life faculty controls the other kamma-born matter in its own group by keeping it alive. And the mental faculties control the citta and factors they arise with, and the matter arising with them. The Manual counts fifteen mental faculties, which reduce to eight factors: life, consciousness, feeling, faith, energy, mindfulness, one-pointedness and wisdom.

Two faculties are left out. Femininity and masculinity are counted among the twenty-two faculties, but they are not faculty conditions, because a condition must produce, support or maintain something, and the sex faculties only shape. Faculty is close to predominance and should not be confused with it: many faculties work together, each in its own sphere, while only one predominant leads the whole citta. The suttas make a related point: the five sense faculties each have their own field, and mind is their common resort.

What conditions
the five sensitivities; the material life faculty; and the mental faculties: life, consciousness, feeling, faith, energy, mindfulness, one-pointedness and wisdom
What is conditioned
the ten sense consciousnesses with their seven universal factors; the other kamma-born matter of the life faculty's group; and the cittas, factors and matter arising with the mental faculties

An example. Reading in poor light with tired eyes: the weakened eye-sensitivity limits how clearly seeing sees. A moment later, mindfulness governs its own task, keeping the line in view, while wisdom governs understanding.

Its kinds

  • prenascence faculty. Each sensitivity, arisen earlier, controls its own sense consciousness.
  • material life faculty. The life faculty controls the kamma-born matter of its group by keeping it alive.
  • conascence faculty. The mental faculties control the mind and matter arising with them.

A panel of ministers, each governing his own province and leaving the others alone.

Simile, after CMA VIII §24 Guide

Not to be confused with

  • predominance. Several faculties control at once, each in its own domain. A predominant leads the whole citta, and only one can do so at a time.
  • the sex faculties. They are faculties but not faculty conditions: they shape the body without producing, supporting or maintaining anything.

Which way it works

  • matter → mind. The five sense faculties, eye and the rest, govern the five kinds of sense consciousness.
  • matter → matter. The physical life faculty sustains the kamma-born matter it is part of. The two sex faculties are not faculty conditions: they shape the body but do none of a condition's three works, producing, supporting or maintaining (Guide to VIII §24; Vism XVII §609).
  • mind → mind. The mental faculties, such as mindfulness and wisdom, each govern their own domain in the citta.
  • mind → matter. The mental faculties also govern the matter that their citta produces.
