Association

sampayutta

Association is the help mental states give one another by sharing a base, an object, an arising and a ceasing.

Direction

Group

Manual

In depth

Association condition, sampayutta-paccayasampayutta · association Association is the help mental states give one another by sharing a base, an object, an arising and a ceasing., is the help mental states give one another by being joined in one unit. A citta and its factors arise together, cease together, take one object, and, where there is matter, rest on one base. These four marks of association are what make them a single moment of experience rather than several. Each state helps the others by being associated with them in this way.

The conditioning and conditioned states are exactly those of mutuality among mental states: every citta and factor, for all the others in the same moment. But the force is different. Mutuality stresses that help is returned; association stresses that the states are fused, blended so that they cannot be pulled apart.

Association is strictly mental. Matter is never associated with anything, not even the mind-born matter that arises with a citta, because it does not share the citta's object and it does not cease with it. That relation, across the divide between mind and matter, is the work of the opposite condition, dissociation. Newcomers who picture the factors of a citta as separate agents cooperating should note what association implies: they are not parts that meet, but aspects of one event.

What conditions
every citta and mental factor
What is conditioned
the other cittas and factors associated with it in the same moment

An example. Seeing a friend across the street: the seeing citta, contact, feeling, perception, volition, attention and the rest arise together, cease together, know the same visible form and rest on the same eye; each helps the others as associated.

Not to be confused with

  • mutuality. The same mental states, but mutuality also covers the great elements and the heart-base at rebirth. Association covers only mind.
  • dissociation. Its counterpart: help between mind and matter, which can never be associated.
