Decisive support

upanissaya

Decisive support is strong help, often from far away: a good friend, a climate, a past habit, a resolve, even Nibbāna as an object.

Direction
Anything → mind
Synthesis
one of the four: decisive support
Group

Manual
VIII §§11–28

In depth

Decisive support condition, upanissaya-paccayaupanissaya · decisive support a strong earlier cause, such as a habit, a teacher or a climate, is strong help. The prefix upa- intensifies: this is not a mere foundation but a powerful reason on which the conditioned states heavily depend. It is one of the four conditions into which the Manual folds all twenty-four, and it is the widest way in which the past shapes the present.

It has three kinds. Object decisive support is an object held in high regard, on which the mind that takes it strongly depends; its states are those of object predominance. Proximity decisive support is the citta that has just ceased, seen as strongly needed by the next; its states are those of proximity. Natural decisive support covers anything in the past, mental or material, that has become strong enough to help later cittas: lust that leads to theft, faith that leads to giving, good health that brings happiness and energy, illness that brings sorrow, a person, food, a season, a dwelling. Past kamma, too, supports its results in this way when it is strong.

The conditioned states are always mental: later cittas and their factors. Matter can be a decisive support, but cannot be decisively supported. Natural decisive support does not respect kinds: an unwholesome past can support a wholesome present, as remorse can lead to resolve.

What conditions
esteemed objects; the citta and factors just ceased; and strong past cittas, factors, any of the 28 kinds of matter, and some concepts
What is conditioned
later cittas and their factors

An example. Someone who once spent months with a kind and steady teacher finds, years later, that the memory moves them to patience in a quarrel. The teacher, and their own past faith, are natural decisive supports for that patience.

Its kinds

  • object decisive support. An object given weight, on which the mind strongly depends; the same states as object predominance.
  • proximity decisive support. The citta just ceased, strongly needed by the next; the same states as proximity.
  • natural decisive support. Anything from the past, internal or external, strong enough to help later states, including kamma for its results.

Not to be confused with

  • support. Support is a present foundation, such as the eye for seeing. Decisive support is a strong reason, usually from earlier.
  • repetition. Repetition strengthens the next javana in the same process. A habit built over years is natural decisive support.

Connections

Which way it works

  • concepts → mind. Some concepts, such as a person, can be a natural decisive support: a good teacher, met long ago, still inspires faith.
  • mind → mind. Faith, a past resolve, or a habit of anger strongly supports later states; each citta also strongly supports the next.
  • matter → mind. Climate, food and lodging can decisively support good or bad states of mind.

Stories that show it

  • Where the Abhidhamma was first taught: that hearing, lives ago, supported learning it again
  • Assaji's four lines: a stranger's verse is the decisive support for awakening
  • The rose-apple tree: the remembered jhāna, as decisive support for the path to awakening
  • The whole of the holy life: a good friend: a person as natural decisive support for the path
  • Yasa wakes in the night: the commentary: foulness practised in a past life was the decisive support
