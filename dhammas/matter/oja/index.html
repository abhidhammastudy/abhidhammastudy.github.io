Nutritive essence

ojā

Nutritive essence: the nourishment in matter. It is one of the eight inseparables, and taken as food it is one of the four origins of matter.

Origins

Manual

In depth

Nutritive essence, ojāojā · nutritive essence the essence in food, and in every kalāpa, that sustains matter, is the nourishing quality in matter. The Manual lists it under edible food, kabaḷīkāra āhāra, whose characteristic is this essence; it keeps the body going, and shows as the body made stronger. Gross food is only its carrier. It is concrete matter and one of the eight inseparables, present in every group and arising from all four origins.

Ojā has two jobs. As an origin of matter, the essence of food that has been swallowed, on reaching its moment of presence, produces new nutriment-born groups; the essence in each of these produces another, so that one meal starts a chain of octads, which the commentaries say repeats ten or twelve times. The essence already inside groups of every origin also supports its own group and the groups around it. As a condition, it is material nutriment: it produces, maintains and props up the body, as a prop holds up an old house.

The commentaries add that one day's food can support the body for up to seven days, and that a mother's food nourishes the embryo. In the fine-material world there are no nutriment-born groups, though ojā is still present in every group there as an inseparable.

The Buddha told of parents crossing a desert who, with no other food, ate the flesh of their dead son only to reach the far side. Food is to be taken in that spirit: for crossing, not for pleasure.

Simile, after SN 12.63

Not to be confused with

  • taste. Taste is known by the tongue. Nutritive essence is not tasted; it is what sustains.
  • the nutriment condition. Ojā is a kind of matter. Nutriment condition is the Paṭṭhāna's name for the way it, and three mental nutriments, help what they sustain.

In your own experience. Notice the change in the body an hour after a meal: strength returning, warmth, heaviness or ease. That change is nutriment-born matter at work.
