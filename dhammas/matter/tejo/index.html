Matter

Fire

tejo

The fire element: heat and cold. It ripens and matures matter, and as temperature it is one of the four origins of matter.

Origins
kamma, mind, temperature, nutriment
Kind
concrete
Group
Great elements
Manual
VI §§2–15

In depth

The fire element, tejo-dhātutejo · fire element heat and cold; it matures matter and, as temperature, produces it, is heat. Cold is not its absence but a low degree of it, so both are experienced through fire. Its function is to ripen or mature the matter around it, and the commentaries give its manifestation as a steady supply of softness, as heat softens what it touches. It is concrete, one of the four great elements, one of the eight inseparables, and part of the tangible.

Fire has a second role that the other elements lack. Under the name temperature, utuutu · temperature the fire element as a producer of matter, inside and outside the body, it is one of the four origins of matter. Once it reaches its moment of presence, the fire element in any group can produce new matter, inside the body and outside it, from the growth of a plant to the change of the weather. In a living being this begins at the presence moment of rebirth-linking; after death, temperature-born matter is all that continues, as the corpse.

The suttas name the fire within the body as what warms it, what ages it, what burns in fever, and what digests food. Newcomers tend to think of fire as flame; in the Abhidhamma a block of ice has its fire element too.

Not to be confused with

  • temperature as an origin of matter. These are the same dhamma in two roles. As the fire element it is a quality of its group; as temperature it is that quality producing a fresh group.
  • the feeling of heat. Heat is matter. Liking or disliking it is feeling, a mental factor that arises in the mind which knows the heat.

In your own experience. Notice the warmth of the hands resting together, then the coolness of air at the nostrils. Both are the fire element, at different strengths, and neither stays the same for long.
