In depth

Production, upacayaupacaya · production Production: the first arising of a material series, as when the body's first groups arise at conception., is the start of a material series: the moment it is set going. With continuity it is one of two names for the birth, jātijāti · birth the arising of the aggregates in a new existence; the eleventh link, and the characteristic of matter called production, of matter. The Manual's Guide gives the standard example: the body, sex and heart-base decads arising at conception are production; the same groups arising again throughout life are continuity.

Production is non-concrete. It is one of the four characteristics of matter, which mark what matter goes through rather than adding a new kind of thing. Two consequences follow. It has no origin of its own: the Manual says the characteristics are not produced by any of the four origins, because their very nature is to be the producing, ageing and passing of what is produced. And it is not a member of any material group: the characteristics only indicate, as space only delimits.

The commentaries match the three phases of a material instant to the characteristics: birth to arising, decay to presence, impermanence to dissolution. A newcomer may ask why birth is counted as matter at all. The answer is practical. Seeing that matter has a beginning, every time, is the first step to seeing that it has an end.

Picture a pit dug beside a river. The water first welling into it, and then filling it, is like production; the water overflowing and running on, the same flow renewed, is like continuity.

Simile, after Visuddhimagga XIV

Not to be confused with

  • continuity. Both are the birth of matter. Production is the first arising of a series; continuity is every later arising in the same series.
  • the origins of matter. Kamma, mind, temperature and nutriment produce matter. Production is not one of them; it is the mark of matter being produced.
