    How we talk here

    • Ask and answer about the Abhidhamma and its practice; keep to the page's subject where there is one.
    • Be kind. Readers come from many traditions and none, and many are beginners.
    • Name your sources (the Manual by chapter and §, a sutta, a commentary) and say when something is your own view.
    • No advertising, no personal attacks, and nothing you would not want shown under your name.
    • Use Report for anything that breaks these; an admin may hide a comment or stop an account from writing.
