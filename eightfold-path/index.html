The noble eightfold path, factor by factor

One short page for each of the eight factors: what it is in a sentence, its training, and the mental factor it is in the Abhidhamma's terms. Start with any one; each page stands on its own.

The eight factors, one page each

Wisdom paññā · how we see, and what we aim at

  1. 1 Right view sammā-diṭṭhi Seeing things as they are, starting from the four truths: suffering, its cause, its ending and the way.
  2. 2 Right intention sammā-saṅkappa Turning the mind toward letting go, goodwill and not harming.

Virtue sīla · what we hold back from

  1. 3 Right speech sammā-vācā Holding back from lies, divisive talk, harsh words and idle chatter.
  2. 4 Right action sammā-kammanta Holding back from killing, stealing and sexual misconduct.
  3. 5 Right livelihood sammā-ājīva Holding back from earning a living in ways that harm or deceive.

Concentration samādhi · how we train the mind

  1. 6 Right effort sammā-vāyāma Working to drop what is unwholesome and keep it away, and to grow what is wholesome and keep it.
  2. 7 Right mindfulness sammā-sati Staying present with what is here, in body, feelings, mind and mental objects, without drifting off.
  3. 8 Right concentration sammā-samādhi A mind settled and unified on one object; the suttas describe it as the four meditative absorptions (jhāna).
