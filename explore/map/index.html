      How this map is made

      Drawn from the site's own pages and the links its content already holds. Pages that link closely sit close; the ten areas are grouped from the links and named by hand. Positions are fixed in advance, so the map does not reshuffle between visits.

      Colour shows the area a page belongs to or, if you switch it, the kind of thing: indigo for mind, amber for matter, teal for Nibbāna alone, an outline for teaching pages (readings, stories, concepts), a diamond for the conditions; a red ring marks the unwholesome factors. Nibbāna stands apart, beyond the edge of the conditioned map: it is the unconditioned and is never a place, a centre or a summit. Only thin rays reach it, from the pages on the minds that take it as object.

      Sizes and heights measure this site's writing and links. They are not a ranking of importance in the Dhamma.

      A study companion to Bhikkhu Bodhi's A Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma, which it retells and cites but does not reproduce. Every page names its sources; the readings of stories are marked as study aids.
