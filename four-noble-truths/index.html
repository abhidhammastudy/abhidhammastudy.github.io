The four noble truths, one by one

One short page for each truth: what it is in a sentence, its task, and what it is in the Abhidhamma's terms. Start with any one; each page stands on its own.

The four truths, one page each

  1. 1 Suffering dukkha More than pain: birth, ageing and death, and everything we can cling to, pleasant things included, comes from conditions and passes away. to be fully understood · pariññeyya
  2. 2 The origin of suffering samudaya Its cause is craving: the thirst for pleasant things, for being, and for not being, which keeps life after life going. to be abandoned · pahātabba
  3. 3 The cessation of suffering nirodha When craving fades and ends completely, suffering ends too: that ending is Nibbāna, the one reality not made by causes. to be realised · sacchikātabba
  4. 4 The path to its cessation magga The way to that ending is the noble eightfold path: right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration. to be developed · bhāvetabba
