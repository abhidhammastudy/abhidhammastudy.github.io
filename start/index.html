Where to begin

A guide for the first visit. No knowledge of the Abhidhamma is needed: each reading starts from experience and explains its terms as it goes. Here is what the site holds, a short first path, and the whole journey in seven levels.

Continue: Begin the first path

What is here

  • Readings Short essays, one idea each, with a drawn figure. The heart of the site; read them in order.
  • Concepts Eighteen key ideas, each on one page, to look up while reading.
  • Dhammas Every mental factor, kind of matter and condition, with how they connect.
  • Glossary Every Pāli word on the site. Dotted words anywhere show their meaning on hover or tap.
  • Stories The Buddha's life, the Jātakas and the disciples, each linked to an idea.
  • Explore The concept map and 3D models to turn and take apart.
  • The book Everything in one document, to read offline or print.

Your first path · 86 minutes

Ten readings, about an hour and a half, that give the shape of the whole: what experience is made of, how a moment of it unfolds, how it carries on, and how it ends. Read one a sitting. Then come back to the full journey and fill in the rest.

  2. A map of the eighty-nineThe kinds of consciousness, and the map the rest of the site uses.8 min

  4. How matter is madeThe other half of experience, matter, as brief events.8 min
  5. Mind and matter togetherHow mind and matter hold each other up, moment by moment.14 min
  6. What each citta doesThe jobs a citta can do and the doors it works at: the words the next reading needs.6 min

  10. The four noble truthsWhat the analysis is for: the first sermon's frame, read as a sorting of realities, and the way out.9 min

How to read

  • Read one reading a sitting, slowly. Each is under a quarter of an hour.
  • Dotted words show their meaning when you point at or tap them; every term is also in the glossary.
  • Write your own answer to the question at the end before opening the one given.
  • The figures can be scrolled and zoomed; the 3D models can be turned by dragging.
  • Each reading cites the section of Bhikkhu Bodhi's Comprehensive Manual it follows, if you want to read the source alongside.
  • The book has everything in one document, to read offline or print.

Ticks show the readings you have opened. They are kept only in this browser.
